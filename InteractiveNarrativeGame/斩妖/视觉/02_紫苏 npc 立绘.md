# 《凶宅·西京红衣怨》角色立绘 Prompt 集

  

> 美术方向：凄美、哀伤、留白感强，避免恐怖片式的惊悚构图（无獠牙、无扭曲肢体、无

> 正面惊吓表情）。紫苏作为"厉鬼"，视觉上走"美人蒙冤而死、凄怆动人"的路线，而非

> "吓人的鬼"。整体色调偏冷、偏旧，像一张被岁月和执念泡旧的老照片。

>

> 命名规范沿用 renpy-npc-character-system skill：主角立绘走 `fs_daoshi_male_cover`

> （角色创建选择用大图）与 `daoshi_male_normal` / `daoshi_male_battle`（对话/战斗

> 立绘）两套命名；NPC 无需门派前缀，直接用角色拼音或英文标识即可，具体见每个角色

> 条目下的建议文件名。

## 一、法师（主角，道士门派）

  

**建议文件名**：`fs_daoshi_male_cover`（创建界面大图）／`daoshi_male_normal`（对话常态）／`daoshi_male_battle`（对峙/施法状态）

  

### 通用 Prompt（常态，用于对话立绘）

  

```

Chinese xianxia/daoist exorcist, young adult male, full body character portrait,

transparent background, standing pose facing slightly to the side.

Wearing a weathered dark indigo Daoist robe with faded talisman patterns along

the hem, a worn oilcloth satchel slung across one shoulder, sleeves slightly

rolled as if from long travel. Hair tied back in a simple topknot, a few loose

strands framing a tired but composed face. Expression calm, watchful, slightly

melancholic — the look of someone used to carrying other people's grief.

Holds a peachwood sword loosely at his side, not raised, not threatening.

Muted color palette: ink blue, faded brown, pale grey, a single small red

talisman-cord accent for a hint of warmth. Soft rim lighting, painterly

semi-realistic style, elegant linework, restrained and dignified mood —

like a lone traveler carved out of an old ink-wash scroll. No blood, no gore,

no aggressive combat pose.

```

  

### 战斗/对峙立绘 Prompt（`daoshi_male_battle`）

  

```

Same character as above (young Daoist exorcist), full body, transparent

background, dynamic but controlled stance — mid-step, one hand forming a

hand seal (jueyin), the other holding a peachwood sword raised to guard

position. Robe sleeves and hem caught mid-motion by a light gust, a few

talisman papers fanned between his fingers glowing with a faint pale-green

ember light. Expression focused and resolute, not angry or feral. Lighting:

a single cool moonlit rim-light against a dark negative space, emphasizing

silhouette and motion rather than violence. Painterly semi-realistic style,

same color palette as the normal pose (ink blue, faded brown, pale grey),

elegant and cinematic, no visible wounds, no gore, no monstrous distortion.

```

## 二、紫苏（红衣女鬼，核心角色）

  

> 视觉核心：凄美而非恐怖。血泪是"哀伤的印记"而不是"恐怖特效"，风衣的红是

> "褪色的旧红"而不是"鲜血刺目的红"，整体要让人心疼，而不是让人害怕。

  

**建议文件名**：`zisu_normal`（回忆/温和场景）／`zisu_ghost`（厉鬼形态，主要形态）／`zisu_ending_peaceful`（若做温情结局立绘，可选）

  

### 厉鬼形态 Prompt（主要形态，对应原文"她悬在半空"）

  

```

Ethereal Chinese ghost woman, young adult, full body, floating pose, feet not

touching the ground, transparent background. Wearing a long faded crimson

windbreaker-style coat, fabric flowing weightlessly as if underwater rather

than violently whipped by wind — soft, mournful movement, not aggressive.

Long black hair drifting loosely around her, a few strands across her face.

Pale, translucent skin with a soft ethereal glow rather than a sickly pallor.

Two thin, delicate tear-tracks of faded dark red beneath her eyes, subtle,

like old watercolor stains rather than fresh blood — evoking sorrow, not

horror. Her expression is one of profound exhaustion and grief that has

already burned past anger into quiet devastation — downcast eyes, soft

furrowed brow, lips slightly parted as if about to speak but giving up.

No visible teeth, no distorted features, no aggressive posture. Cool

moonlight palette: faded crimson, deep indigo shadow, pale silver-blue

ambient glow, a few embers of soft green-white light drifting around her

like dying fireflies. Painterly, semi-realistic, romantic-tragic mood —

like a portrait of grief given form. Elegant, dignified, beautiful despite

sorrow. No gore, no exposed wounds, no horror-movie framing.

```

  

### 温和/回忆形态 Prompt（对应"她生前的样子"，用于闪回或幻境场景）

  

```

Young Chinese woman, full body, gentle standing pose, transparent background.

Same identity as the ghost form but alive and warm — wearing a simple period

qipao or long tunic in soft muted rose-red, hair neatly pinned with a small

plain hairpin, a faint genuine smile with warmth reaching her eyes, holding

a small cloth-wrapped notebook against her chest as if protecting something

precious. Soft daylight lighting, warm but still slightly muted color

palette (dusty rose, cream, soft brown), painterly semi-realistic style,

evoking quiet resolve and hidden sadness beneath calm composure. No ghost

elements, no glow, fully corporeal and lifelike.

```

## 九、通用美术规范（适用于以上所有角色）

  

- **统一比例与站位**：所有立绘按 `renpy-npc-character-system` skill 里的

  `char_left` / `char_center` / `char_right` transform 使用等比缩放（`fit "contain"`），

  故生成图片时建议主体占画面纵向 80%～90%，头顶留白，脚部完整入镜，避免生成时

  主体贴满全图导致后期缩放时头部被裁切。

- **透明背景**：所有立绘均需透明背景（PNG），便于叠加到场景背景之上。

- **色调基调**：整体项目建议统一在"冷雾蓝＋褪色暖棕"的双色调体系里波动，紫苏

  的红是全案唯一被允许"跳出冷色调"的强调色，用于视觉焦点，其余角色的服装/配色

  都刻意压低饱和度，避免抢镜。

- **禁止元素**（全角色通用）：不出现獠牙、爪状变形肢体、外露伤口/器官、大面积

  鲜红色血液喷溅、扭曲挣扎的肢体动作、正面尖叫/怒吼表情。恐怖感应通过"眼神里

  的绝望"和"氛围光影"传达，而不是通过生理性恐怖细节传达。
